## Musicals

Added on 1 October 2026 from Will's three requested favourites. These entries do not claim attendance, a viewing date, or a particular cast. Will subsequently supplied the favourite songs: "The Phantom of the Opera" for Phantom, "When I Grow Up" for Matilda, and "Regardez-moi" for Molière. The short introductions paraphrase production descriptions, not personal reviews. Existing songs, albums, lyrics and concert records are unchanged.

- **The Phantom of the Opera**: [official production site](https://www.phantomoftheopera.com/) and [the theatre owner's show page](https://lwtheatres.co.uk/whats-on/the-phantom-of-the-opera/) for Andrew Lloyd Webber's stage musical, Christine and the Phantom beneath the Paris Opera House. The image is a historical stage poster, not the film poster or a claim about a performance Will attended: [poster catalogue](https://www.affiches-et-posters.com/musique-at-6/comedies-musicales-t-6_232_1781.html), [asset](https://images.affiches-et-posters.com/albums/3/3066/affiche-comedie-musicale-fantome-l-opera-broadway--881.jpg).
- **Matilda The Musical**: [the production's official introduction](https://uk.matildathemusical.com/about/) for Roald Dahl's story, Dennis Kelly's book, Tim Minchin's songs, and Matilda's resistance to the adults around her. Stage poster reproduced on [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/shows/Matilda-The-Musical-334293.html), [asset](https://cloudimages.broadwayworld.com/logos/305.jpeg); not artwork for the 2022 film.
- **Molière, le spectacle musical**: [official site](https://moliere-spectacle.com/) identifies Dove Attia's French-language production. [M6's programme description](https://www.m6.fr/moliere-le-spectacle-musical-p_26822) and [the production's digital programme](https://moliere-69b25e.webflow.io/) support the short account of Poquelin forming a theatre company with Madeleine Béjart. [Blue-and-gold artwork](https://moliere-spectacle.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/LOGO-MOLIERE-FOND-BLEU-min-scaled.jpg) comes from the official site's media library.
