# Reading room: complete Song ci texts

The four poems on `/reading/` are complete public-domain works selected by Will. Their modern introductions, analyses, and translations have not been copied. The existing four novel introductions remain unchanged.

The transcriptions consistently follow the poem texts in the digital edition of Long Yusheng’s 《唐宋名家词选》 on his memorial website, checked on 2026-09-12. Punctuation is retained; display line breaks follow the phrasing, and the upper/lower stanza division is preserved as a blank line in content and separate paragraphs in HTML.

## Version decisions

- The Su Shi text retains “馀生”; the Chen Yuyi text retains “二十馀年”, as printed in the selected source. “余” is the familiar normalized form.
- The Wang Anshi text follows this source’s “无奈被它情担阁” and “当初谩留华表语”; other editions use “他” and/or “漫”. These are not edits to Will’s personal writing.
- The Li Qingzhao text follows the revised text’s “学诗漫有惊人句”. Its source explicitly records the first edition’s variant “谩有”. It retains “鹏正举” and “吹取”.
- All four have two stanzas. Nothing is elided and no English translation or personal reflection is invented.
